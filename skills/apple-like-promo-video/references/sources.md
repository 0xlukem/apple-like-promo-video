# Reference provenance

Use references to understand decisions, then author original copy, compositions,
and media for the current subject. None of these sources supplies a universal
Apple easing curve, font requirement, loudness target, or fixed editing formula.

## Visual reference

- [Leo's Apple promo analysis](https://x.com/leomeethewoo/status/2098032450731720823)
  is a third-party analysis, not an Apple production manual. Browser samples
  inspected during research included large imperative typography, selective
  luminous emphasis, app and device continuity, and detail-to-product reveals.
  The embedded audio was not directly auditioned during that research. Exact
  animation curves and audio synchronization must not be attributed to that inspection.

## Production accounts

- [Antfood: Apple iMac Pro](https://www.antfood.com/work/apple-imac): the production
  studio describes closely scored music and sound for fast, irregular animation,
  with a synthesizer-driven beat expressing creative power.
- [Amp.Amsterdam: Playgrounds In Motion](https://amp.amsterdam/work/playgrounds-in-motion-2023/):
  a non-Apple example of scene-specific sonic direction and music/effects sharing
  rhythmic and melodic roles.
- [Zelig Sound: process](https://www.zeligsound.com/process): a studio's approach
  to brand-led sonic concepts, contextual testing, and reusable sound resources.

The gesture pairings and provisional timing ranges in `direction.md` are design
proposals derived for this workflow. When adding references, label what was
directly observed, documented by a source, measured, or proposed. Record actual
timecodes only when the referenced media was available for inspection.
