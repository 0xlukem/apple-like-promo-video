## Review and delivery

Record pass, failure, or not reviewed **with evidence** for each relevant check:

- Message and hierarchy over time; text fit and reading time at delivery size.
- Editorial review from direction.md: weak/unclear beats and corrections made.
- Featured image/product motion: action and resolved-state evidence; purposeful holds.
- Selected controls versus actual scene outcomes, including declared exceptions.
- Motion paths, transition continuity, pacing, and complete normal-speed playback.
- Audiovisual cue alignment; mix, silence, repetition, voice clarity, and tails.
- Essential meaning muted; audio-only review; listening devices actually used.
- Final resources, resolved fonts, render errors, and visual artifacts.
- Output duration, dimensions, FPS, codec, audio stream, and destination settings.

List files delivered, reproducible preview/render commands, known limitations,
and the approval state. Separate automated measurements from perceptual review.
For feedback, record the issue, correction, other affected beats, and recheck result.
